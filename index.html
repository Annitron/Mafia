Mafia Game - Rules & Roles

Objective

Town wins: All Mafia members are eliminated.

Mafia wins: Mafia equals or outnumbers town members.

Game Flow

Day Phase: All players discuss and vote to eliminate someone. The player with the most votes is eliminated.

Night Phase: Mafia members choose their target. Special roles perform their night abilities.

Town Roles (Villager Faction)

  • Sheriff: Investigates players each night to learn their faction.
  • Doctor: Protects one player each night from Mafia elimination.
  • Deputy: Investigates players at night (only if Sheriff dies).
  • Bodyguard: Guards a player to protect them from attacks.
  • Mayor: Can reveal their identity and gain an extra vote during day phases.
  • Medium: Can speak with dead players during day phase.
  • Veteran: Can activate an alert during the day; anyone visiting them at night dies.
  • Gaoler: Can jail a player during the day to prevent their day abilities.
  • Undertaker: Learns the role of the player who was eliminated during the day.

Mafia Roles (Mafia Faction)

  • Framer: Can frame a player during the day, making them appear as Mafia to investigators.
  • Godfather: Can get double the votes during a round, but multiple uses may reveal their identity.
  • Imposter: Can disguise as a Villager during the day.
  • Kidnapper: Can kidnap a player during the day.

Controls

Movement: WASD keys to move your circle around the town.

Voting: Hover over a house to highlight it. Press F or click Vote to vote for that player.

Abilities: Press E or click Ability to use your role's special power (if available).

Skip Phase: Press Q or click Skip to vote to end the phase early.

Chat: Main chat visible to all. Dead/Mafia chat available based on your status.

Rules: Press R to toggle this rules panel.

Tips

- Pay attention to voting patterns and player behavior.

- Mafia may try to blend in; use investigations wisely.

- Dead players can only chat with each other and the Medium.
